WenQuanYi Micro Hei (文泉驿微米黑)
================================

Bundled font file: assets/fonts/wqy-microhei.ttc
Purpose: net-tools embeds this font as a CJK fallback so that Chinese and other
CJK text renders correctly regardless of the fonts installed on the host system.
